About Us

Lakshmi Advance Biotech

Manufacturers of organic fertilizers, pesticides, micronutrients and micro fertilizers for Indian agriculture.

Who we are

Lakshmi Advance Biotech (LABH) manufactures organic agricultural fertilizers and crop care products designed for stronger plant growth, better nutrient uptake and dependable field performance. Our formulations are prepared under expert supervision and tested across multiple crops and growing conditions.

Our portfolio spans organic inputs, insecticides, herbicides, plant growth promoters, micronutrient mixes and single-element micro fertilizers – giving farmers a complete crop-care basket from a single trusted source.

What we stand for

  • Quality first – every batch tested for performance
  • Farmer-friendly pricing and pack sizes
  • Strong dealer and technical support network
  • Focus on soil health and sustainable productivity
