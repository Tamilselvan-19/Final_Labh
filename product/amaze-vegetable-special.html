About Amaze Vegetable Special

Multi-micronutrient mix designed for vegetable crops. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Balanced blend of Zn, Fe, Mn, Cu, B and Mo in chelated form
  • Formulation: Water soluble micronutrient mix

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Tomato, brinjal, chilli and capsicum
  • Cucurbits – gourd, melon, cucumber
  • Leafy vegetables and beans
  • Onion, garlic and root vegetables

Key benefits

  • Corrects multiple micronutrient deficiencies
  • Improves flowering and fruit setting
  • Enhances fruit size, colour and shelf life
  • Quick foliar absorption

Dosage

  • 2 – 3 g per litre of water as foliar spray.

Method of application

  • Spray at vegetative, flowering and fruit setting stages for best results.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
