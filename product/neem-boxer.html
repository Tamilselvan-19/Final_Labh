Neem Boxer

Neem oil based botanical insecticide for organic farms.

About Neem Boxer

Neem oil based botanical insecticide for organic farms. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Neem oil (Azadirachtin)
  • Formulation: Botanical oil concentrate

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Vegetables – sucking pests, leaf miner
  • Fruit crops – mealy bug, scale
  • Pulses – aphids, white fly
  • Floriculture – thrips, mites

Key benefits

  • Repellent and antifeedant action
  • Disrupts insect growth and moulting
  • Safe for beneficial insects and pollinators
  • Suitable for organic and IPM programmes

Dosage

  • 3 – 5 ml per litre of water (1.0 – 1.5 litres per acre).

Method of application

  • Spray on the under-surface of leaves preferably in the evening. Repeat at 7–10 day intervals.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
