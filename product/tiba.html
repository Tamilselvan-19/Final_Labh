Triiodobenzoic Acid — Embryo Formation From Embryo Genic Cells

About TIBA

Triiodobenzoic Acid — Embryo Formation From Embryo Genic Cells. Manufactured and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Technical Name: Triiodobenzoic Acid
  • Type: Embryo Formation From Embryo Genic Cells
  • Mode of Action: Development of Shoot,Root and Embryos

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Major Crops: All Crops
  • Crop recommended: cotton, all vegetables,fruits, flower, pulses, soyabean paddy, wheate, maize( makka), sugarcan, and all crop

Method of application

  • Application: spray
  • Usage: spay after fertigation,plant growth stage , for better result use 15/20 days interval in one crop in twise

Details

  • Doses: 50ml/15 ltr water

Key benefits

  • TIBA treated plants were more resistant to lodging than untreated plants.
  • TIBA treatments reduced plant height, partially inhibited apical dominance, and produced a triangular shaped canopy with a more vertical leaf orientation.
  • During the cucumber fruiting period lengthen the fruit and promote fruit expansion
