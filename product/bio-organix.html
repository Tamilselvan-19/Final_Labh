About Bio Organix

Organic bio-stimulant with micronutrients and amino acids. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Seaweed extract, humic acid, amino acids and chelated micronutrients
  • Formulation: Liquid bio-stimulant

Recommended uses & target crops

  • All field and horticulture crops
  • Vegetables, fruits and flowers
  • Plantation crops and turf

Key benefits

  • Boosts root growth and nutrient uptake
  • Improves stress tolerance – drought, heat, salinity
  • Enhances flowering, fruit setting and quality
  • 100% soluble – fits drip and foliar systems

Dosage

  • 2 – 3 ml per litre of water as foliar spray; 1 litre per acre via drip.

Method of application

  • Apply at vegetative, flowering and fruiting stages.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
