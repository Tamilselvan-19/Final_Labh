RockStar

Cypermethrin 5% EC + Chlorpyriphos 50% EC combination.

About RockStar

Cypermethrin 5% EC + Chlorpyriphos 50% EC combination. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Cypermethrin 5% EC + Chlorpyriphos 50% EC
  • Formulation: EC combination

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Cotton – bollworm complex, sucking pests
  • Paddy – stem borer, leaf folder
  • Pulses – pod borer
  • Vegetables – fruit and shoot borer

Key benefits

  • Dual mode of action – pyrethroid + organophosphate
  • Controls sucking and chewing pests in one spray
  • Quick knockdown plus longer residual cover
  • Reduces number of sprays per season

Dosage

  • 1.5 – 2.0 ml per litre of water (400 – 500 ml per acre).

Method of application

  • Apply as foliar spray with uniform coverage. Avoid spraying during peak flowering for bee safety.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
