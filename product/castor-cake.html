About Castor Cake

Castor de-oiled cake — a traditional organic manure for fertility build-up. Manufactured and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: De-oiled castor seed cake
  • Formulation: Crushed cake / powder

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Vegetables and floriculture
  • Banana, papaya, sugarcane
  • Coconut and arecanut

Key benefits

  • Rich source of organic N, P and K
  • Repels soil pests and nematodes
  • Improves soil texture and water holding
  • Builds long-term soil fertility

Dosage

  • 200 – 400 kg per acre as basal application.

Method of application

  • Apply 10–15 days before sowing / transplanting and incorporate into soil.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves and protective clothing while handling.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per local agronomic recommendation.
