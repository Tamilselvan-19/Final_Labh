Boron

Boron micronutrient for flowering and fruit setting.

About Boron

Boron micronutrient for flowering and fruit setting. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Borax / Solubor (10–20% B)
  • Formulation: Water soluble crystals

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Cotton – square and boll retention
  • Groundnut, sunflower and pulses
  • Fruit crops – mango, citrus, apple
  • Vegetables – cauliflower, tomato, brinjal

Key benefits

  • Improves pollen viability and seed setting
  • Reduces flower and fruit drop
  • Strengthens cell walls and fruit quality
  • Corrects hollow-stem and brown-heart disorders

Dosage

  • 4 – 6 kg per acre as basal; 1 – 2 g per litre as foliar spray.

Method of application

  • Apply basally with FYM or spray at flowering and early fruit setting stages.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
