About Fish Amino Acid

Natural amino acid liquid fertilizer prepared from fish for rapid growth. Manufactured and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Fermented fish amino acids and natural enzymes
  • Formulation: Liquid concentrate

Recommended uses & target crops

  • All vegetables and leafy greens
  • Flowering and fruiting crops
  • Plantation and horticulture

Key benefits

  • Supplies readily available organic nitrogen
  • Promotes lush green vegetative growth
  • Improves taste, colour and shelf-life of produce
  • Activates soil microflora

Dosage

  • 3 – 5 ml per litre of water as foliar spray; 1 litre per acre via drip / drench.

Method of application

  • Spray in cool hours of the day; can be combined with regular foliar nutrition schedule.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves and protective clothing while handling.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per local agronomic recommendation.
