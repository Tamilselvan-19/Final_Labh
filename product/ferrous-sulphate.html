About Ferrous Sulphate

Iron micro fertilizer to correct chlorosis in crops. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Ferrous Sulphate Heptahydrate 19% Fe
  • Formulation: Crystalline powder

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Paddy, pulses and oilseeds
  • Vegetables and fruit crops
  • Floriculture and lawns
  • Calcareous and alkaline soils

Key benefits

  • Corrects iron deficiency and interveinal chlorosis
  • Improves green colour and photosynthesis
  • Enhances tiller and root development
  • Boosts yield and crop quality

Dosage

  • 10 – 25 kg per acre as basal; 5 g per litre as foliar spray.

Method of application

  • Apply basally with FYM; foliar spray at early symptom appearance.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
