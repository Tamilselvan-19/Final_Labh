About Panchagavya

Traditional organic liquid for healthy vegetative growth. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Fermented blend of cow dung, cow urine, milk, curd and ghee
  • Formulation: Organic liquid bio-stimulant

Recommended uses & target crops

  • All field and horticulture crops
  • Vegetables, fruits and flowers
  • Pulses, oilseeds and millets
  • Organic and natural farming systems

Key benefits

  • Boosts plant immunity and stress tolerance
  • Improves flowering, fruit setting and quality
  • Activates beneficial microbes in soil
  • Reduces dependence on chemical inputs

Dosage

  • 30 ml per litre of water as foliar spray; 3% solution for soil drench.

Method of application

  • Spray on foliage at 15-day intervals; also use as seed treatment or soil drench.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
