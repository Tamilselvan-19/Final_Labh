About Cyporin-10

Cypermethrin 10% EC – versatile pyrethroid insecticide. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Cypermethrin 10% EC
  • Formulation: Emulsifiable Concentrate (EC)

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Cotton – American bollworm, jassids
  • Vegetables – fruit borer, shoot borer
  • Pulses – pod borer
  • Oilseeds – leaf eating caterpillars

Key benefits

  • Reliable broad-spectrum control
  • Quick knockdown
  • Long residual effect
  • Economical per-acre cost

Dosage

  • 1.0 – 1.5 ml per litre of water (300 – 400 ml per acre).

Method of application

  • Apply as foliar spray; repeat every 10–15 days as required.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
