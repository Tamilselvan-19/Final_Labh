About Seaweed Extract

Liquid organic plant growth promoter derived from natural seaweed. Manufactured and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Ascophyllum nodosum seaweed extract with natural cytokinins and auxins
  • Formulation: Liquid concentrate

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Vegetables, fruits, flowers
  • Cotton, paddy, pulses
  • Plantation and horticulture crops

Key benefits

  • Promotes flowering and fruit setting
  • Improves tolerance to drought and stress
  • Increases chlorophyll content
  • Improves overall plant vigor

Dosage

  • 2 – 3 ml per litre of water as foliar spray.

Method of application

  • Spray during early vegetative, flowering and fruit development stages.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves and protective clothing while handling.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per local agronomic recommendation.
