Press Mud Compost

Decomposed sugarcane press mud rich in organic matter and micronutrients.

About Press Mud Compost

Decomposed sugarcane press mud rich in organic matter and micronutrients. Manufactured and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Composted sugarcane filter press mud enriched with microbes
  • Formulation: Bulk organic compost

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Sugarcane, paddy, banana
  • Vegetables and orchards
  • Reclamation of poor / saline soils

Key benefits

  • Adds large quantities of organic matter
  • Improves soil physical and chemical properties
  • Supplies secondary and micronutrients
  • Helps reclaim sodic / saline soils

Dosage

  • 1 – 2 tonnes per acre as basal application.

Method of application

  • Broadcast and incorporate into soil at land preparation.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves and protective clothing while handling.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per local agronomic recommendation.
