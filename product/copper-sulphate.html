About Copper Sulphate

Copper micronutrient and base for Bordeaux mixture. Designed and supplied by Lakshmi Advance Biotech for dependable field performance.

Composition

  • Active: Copper Sulphate Pentahydrate (24% Cu)
  • Formulation: Crystalline powder

Recommended uses & target crops

  • Paddy, wheat and pulses on copper-deficient soils
  • Vegetables and fruit crops
  • Tea, coffee and plantation crops
  • Used in Bordeaux mixture as fungicide base

Key benefits

  • Corrects copper deficiency – die-back, leaf curl
  • Strengthens cell walls and plant immunity
  • Acts as base for protective fungicide sprays
  • Improves grain and fruit quality

Dosage

  • 4 – 6 kg per acre as basal; 1 – 2 g per litre as foliar spray. For Bordeaux 1% mixture as per standard.

Method of application

  • Apply basally or as foliar spray; prepare Bordeaux fresh, do not store.

Precautions

  • Wear gloves, mask and protective clothing while handling and spraying.
  • Do not eat, drink or smoke during application.
  • Wash hands, face and exposed skin thoroughly after use.
  • Store in original sealed container away from children, food and livestock.
  • Use strictly as per label claim and local agronomic recommendation.
